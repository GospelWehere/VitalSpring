Downtime ProcedureReception prints the next working day's appointment list before closing. During an outage, staff use numbered sheets containing only necessary identity and appointment fields. After restoration, one staff member enters the activity and another verifies it. Clinical paper notes are signed and transferred only by an authorized clinician.

5.2 Paper-Record Migration

  1. Inventory active folders and assign each a migration tracking number.
  2. Identify likely duplicates using hospital number, name, date of birth and phone.
  3. Define which identity, contact, appointment-summary and clinically necessary fields will be captured.
  4. Enter information into a restricted staging area; do not use personal devices.
  5. Have a second person verify identity and critical clinical fields against the folder.
  6. Reconcile counts and investigate every rejected or duplicate row.
  7. Import approved records in controlled batches and record the result.
  8. Retain paper originals for the approved validation period; dispose only through authorized confidential handling.
